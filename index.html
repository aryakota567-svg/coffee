Our Story

From Bean to Cup, with Love.

Founded in 2024, The Daily Grind started as a small passion project among lifelong friends. We believe coffee is more than just a morning caffeine kick—it is a daily ritual that connects communities. We partner directly with sustainable farmers to bring you the highest-grade beans, carefully roasting each batch in-house to unlock perfect, distinctive flavors. Come pull up a chair, take a sip, and make yourself at home.
